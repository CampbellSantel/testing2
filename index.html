No lectures, no grades, just a deadline

Her Take is a weekend jam for women in media, run by students. The brief drops at 6 PM on Friday and everything is due at 6 PM on Sunday.

We bring mentors, studio space, a gear library and too much pizza. You bring ideas, a laptop and maybe a friend.

Questions

Who can join?

Any student who identifies as a woman or as nonbinary. First-years and seniors end up on the same teams, so no experience is needed.

Do I need a team?

No. Come solo and we'll match you at 5 PM on Friday. Teams max out at five people.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Food, studio space and the gear library are free.

What gear can I borrow?

Cameras, lenses, mics, LED panels, a green screen and two VR headsets. First come, first served.

Who owns what we make?

You do. We only ask to screen it at the premiere and post a clip with your credits.

Ship it.

Spots are free and capped at 120 makers. Sign up solo or with a team of up to five.
